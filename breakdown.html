Stay safe while you wait

  1. Switch on your hazard lights.
  2. Move the car as far off the road as you safely can.
  3. On a busy road, wait away from the car on the side away from traffic.
  4. Place your warning triangle (life saver) about 30 metres behind the car.
  5. Keep your phone switched on and nearby.

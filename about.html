Our story

Our background

Founded with a passion for excellence, our garage was created to provide customers with dependable, affordable and high quality vehicle maintenance. Over the years we have earned a reputation for honesty, professionalism and commitment to customer satisfaction.

At Wycliffe Motors we handle a wide range of services including engine diagnostics, brake repairs, oil changes, tyre replacement, car electrical work and general vehicle servicing. Our skilled mechanics use modern tools and techniques to make sure your vehicle runs safely and efficiently.

When your car lets you down on the road, our breakdown team comes to you, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Our vision

To become the most trusted and reliable auto repair service provider in Uganda by delivering quality, affordable and technology-driven vehicle maintenance.

Our mission

To provide exceptional car repair and maintenance through skilled professionals, modern diagnostic tools and a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, so every vehicle leaves our garage in perfect condition.

Our objectives

  • Make it easy for customers to reach us online.
  • Provide clear contact information and communication channels.
  • Show our team, mission and vision for transparency and trust.
  • Promote digital transformation in local automotive services.
